## Managing Cookies

### How to Clear Cookies/Storage

Since we use localStorage (not traditional cookies), here's how to clear it:

**Google Chrome:**
1. Press F12 to open Developer Tools
2. Go to "Application" tab
3. Expand "Local Storage" in left sidebar
4. Click on your site URL
5. Right-click → Clear
6. Or just clear all browsing data: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data

**Firefox:**
1. Press F12 to open Developer Tools
2. Go to "Storage" tab
3. Expand "Local Storage"
4. Right-click on site → Delete All

**Safari:**
1. Safari → Preferences → Privacy
2. Manage Website Data
3. Search for site and Remove

**Microsoft Edge:**
1. Press F12
2. Go to "Application" tab (same as Chrome)
3. Local Storage → Clear

**Note:** Clearing localStorage will log you out of the Service.

### How to Disable Cookies/Storage

**Warning:** Disabling localStorage will prevent you from logging in to the Service.

You can disable storage in your browser settings, but this will make the Service unusable:
- Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Site Settings → Cookies → Block all cookies
- Firefox: Settings → Privacy → Cookies → Block all cookies
- Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies
- Edge: Settings → Cookies → Block all cookies

## Cookie Consent

### Do You Need to Consent?

**No.** Under GDPR and UK privacy law, "strictly necessary" cookies do not require user consent (GDPR Recital 30, ePrivacy Directive Article 5(3)).

Our authentication token is **strictly necessary** because:
- It's required for the Service to function
- You cannot use the Service without it
- It's necessary to perform the contract with you (providing the Service)

### What About the Cookie Banner?

While consent is not legally required for essential cookies, we display an informational banner to:
- Inform you about the authentication token
- Provide transparency about our minimal cookie usage
- Link to this Cookie Policy for more information

Clicking "I Understand" simply acknowledges that you've been informed. It's not a consent mechanism.

> "...the storing of information, or the gaining of access to information already stored, in the terminal equipment of a subscriber or user is only allowed on condition that the subscriber or user concerned has given his or her consent...
