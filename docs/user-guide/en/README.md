## 1. Registration

Getting started with Estate Portal is quick and easy.

### Steps:

1. **Go to the registration page** - Navigate to `/register` or click "Register" in the navigation.

2. **Select your role** - Choose "Estate Agent" as your role. This gives you access to create transactions and manage buyers.

3. **Fill in your details**:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (must be secure)

4. **Check your email** - You'll receive a confirmation email with a verification link.

5. **Verify your account** - Click the link in the email to complete registration.

## 2. First Login

After verifying your email, you're ready to log in.

### Steps:

1. **Go to the login page** - Navigate to `/login`.

2. **Enter your credentials** - Use your registered email and password.

3. **Update password if prompted** - For security, you may be asked to set a new password on first login.

4. **Access your dashboard** - You'll be redirected to your personalized dashboard.

![Login Page](./images/en/02-login-filled.png)

## 3. Account Setup

Personalize your profile and set up your agency branding.

### Profile Settings

1. **Navigate to Settings** - Click on your avatar in the top right and select "Settings".

2. **Upload your profile photo** - Add a professional photo to help buyers recognize you.

3. **Set your preferred language** - Choose from English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, or Polish.

### Branding Setup (Agents Only)

1. **Enter your website URL** - Estate Portal can automatically extract your brand colors from your website.

2. **Click "Extract Colors"** - The system will analyze your website and suggest matching colors.

3. **Upload your agency logo** - This will appear in the header and on client-facing pages.

4. **Customize colors manually** - Fine-tune your primary, secondary, background, and text colors.

![Settings Page](./images/en/04-settings-full.png)

## 4. Create a Transaction

Start tracking a property purchase for your clients.

### Steps:

1. **Click "Create Transaction"** - Find this in the navigation menu or on your dashboard.

2. **Fill in the transaction details**:
   - **Transaction Title** - A descriptive name (e.g., "Villa Toscana Purchase")
   - **Property Address** - The full address of the property
   - **Agent Reference** - Your internal reference number (optional)
   - **Property URL** - Link to the property listing (optional)

3. **Select a milestone template** - Choose a pre-configured template or use the default milestones.

4. **Invite buyers** - Search for existing buyers or enter email addresses to invite new ones.

5. **Click "Create Transaction"** - Your transaction will be created with the selected milestones.

![Create Transaction Form](./images/en/05-create-transaction-filled.png)

### What happens next:

- Transaction is created with milestones
- You are automatically added as a participant
- Invited buyers receive email notifications
- Track progress as the purchase advances

## 5. Manage Buyers

Keep track of all your international buyers in one place.

### Accessing Buyer Management

1. **Go to "Manage Buyers"** - Find this in the navigation menu.

2. **View your buyer list** - See all buyers you've worked with, including their contact information and preferred language.

### Adding a New Buyer

1. **Click "Add Buyer"** - Opens the new buyer form.

2. **Enter buyer details**:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number (optional)
   - Preferred Language

3. **Save the buyer** - They'll be added to your buyer database and can be invited to transactions.

### Inviting Buyers to Transactions

- When creating or editing a transaction, search for buyers by name
- Select buyers from the list to add them as participants
- They'll receive an email invitation to track the transaction

## 6. Milestone Templates

Create reusable templates for common transaction workflows.

### Accessing Templates

1. **Go to "Milestone Templates"** - Find this in the navigation menu.

### Creating a Template

1. **Click "Create Template"** - Opens the template editor.

2. **Name your template** - Give it a descriptive name (e.g., "Standard Italian Purchase").

3. **Add milestones** - Click "Add Milestone" to add steps:
   - Milestone name
   - Description (optional)
   - Expected duration (optional)

4. **Reorder milestones** - Drag and drop to arrange in the correct order.

5. **Save template** - Your template is now available when creating transactions.

### Default Milestones

Estate Portal includes a default template with common milestones:
- Initial Contact
- Property Viewing
- Offer Submitted
- Offer Accepted
- Legal Review
- Survey & Inspection
- Final Contract
- Completion

## 7. Transaction Details

Track progress and communicate with all participants.

### Accessing Transaction Details

1. **Click on any transaction** - From your dashboard or transactions list.

### Available Tabs

#### Tracker Tab
- View all milestones in order
- Mark milestones as complete
- Add notes to milestones
- See completion percentage

#### Messages Tab
- Send messages to all participants
- Messages are translated to each participant's preferred language
- View message history

#### Files Tab
- Upload documents (contracts, inspections, etc.)
- Download shared files
- Organize documents by category

#### Participants Tab
- View all transaction participants
- See each person's role (agent/buyer)
- Invite additional buyers
